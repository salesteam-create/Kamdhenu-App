KKamdhenu Ordering AppClickable prototype for Mohit Ispat

Distributors and dealers book Kamdhenu TMT at today's price.

One app for Mohit Ispat's distributors and dealers to book steel in tonnes, choose a payment term, add sizes and track every truck. Mohit Ispat runs everything from a web admin panel.

Dealer app

For hardware stores supplied by a distributor.

  • Today's price and payment terms
  • Order direct or send to the distributor
  • Full order or lump-sum booking
  • Truck tracking, Received, Pay now
Open dealer app

Distributor app

Same app, plus a Dealers tab.

  • Approve, edit or reject dealer requests
  • Combine them with own stock into one order
  • Add phone orders for dealers not on the app
  • See dealers' direct orders
Open distributor app

Admin panel

For Mohit Ispat, on the web.

  • Publish the daily price by state
  • Confirm orders, add truck dispatches
  • Payments due, received and overdue
  • Dealer list, signups and settings

Live demo: dealer and distributor side by side
