Order genuine Kamdhenu steel from anywhere. The nearest dealer delivers.

A prepaid ordering app for Goa and Karnataka. Customers order TMT bars, binding wire and nails for their site. The order is routed automatically to the nearest Kamdhenu dealer, and the dealer is paid after delivery.

Customer app

For home builders and contractors, including those who live away from their site.

  • Site location and site contact
  • Order by kg or by pieces
  • Prepaid checkout with escrow
  • Tracking, delivery OTP, refund on actual weight
Open customer app

Dealer app

For Kamdhenu dealers. Simple enough to use at the shop counter.

  • New order alerts with a 15 minute window
  • Accept or decline with a reason
  • Loaded weight, invoice and e-way bill
  • Delivery OTP, site photo and payouts
